About Ravi Trading Company: A Legacy of Modern Craftsmanship

The foundation of Ravi Trading Company is built on a deep family tradition of home craftsmanship. Ravi Mistry inherited the expertise and passion for creating exquisite home pieces, skills that were honed by watching and learning from his family's small-scale operations.

In 2004, Ravi Mistry took the ambitious step of bringing that generational knowledge to the wider, competitive urban market. Starting at a very young age, Ravi faced challenging early days with immense perseverance. Today, he is the driving force behind Ravi Trading Company, successfully blending that timeless traditional skill with contemporary design and a clear vision for growth and quality.

Our Core Values

Every product and project is guided by our commitment to:

  • Quality Craftsmanship: Upholding a generational standard of superior construction and lasting quality.
  • Sustainable Sourcing: A conscious focus on ethically sourced and responsible materials.
  • Timeless Design: Creating pieces that remain elegant and desirable for years to come.
  • Customer-First Focus: Ensuring your satisfaction is the measure of our success.

Comprehensive Home Solutions

We are a full-service custom design provider offering a unique suite of services tailored to your exact needs:

  • Bespoke Creation: All products and designs are made precisely as per customer requirement.
  • Renovation & Restoration: We expertly renovate and restore your old furniture and decor.
  • Design Consultation: Work directly with our experts to plan, customize, and refine your projects.
  • Full Service & Installation: We manage the entire process, including professional assembly and installation assistance.
